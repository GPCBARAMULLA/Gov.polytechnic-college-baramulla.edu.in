About the Institution

Government Polytechnic College Baramulla was established in 2012. We are affiliated with JKSBOTE and approved by AICTE. Our mission is to provide industry-based technical education. Our collaboration with TATA Technologies (CIIIT) and NIT Srinagar ensures students are ready for the modern workforce.
